# Code of Conduct

OpenParallel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and make room for people whose first language isn't English. Harassment, insults, sexualised language and publishing other people's private information aren't welcome anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere you represent it.

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Ali-expandings). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks these standards.
